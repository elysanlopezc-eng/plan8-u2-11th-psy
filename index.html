Building a Research Proposal

Subtopic: Aligning question, hypothesis, method, sample, instrument, ethical safeguards, and planned analysis; peer review and revision.

CNB Competency & Learning Outcome:

Designs scientific research proposals applying psychological methodologies, tools, and strict ethical standards.

Montano Habits & Key Skills:
Habit 2: Begin with the End in Mind Habit 4: Think Win-Win Critical Thinking Peer Feedback

Phase I: Review of Research Proposal Parts (5 Minutes)

Diagnostic & Warm-up

Welcome to class! Today we align all structural components of a psychological research proposal. Let's start with a quick brain sparker to recall essential components.

Interactive Flash Activity: "Align the Component"

Click on any core element below to reveal its key requirement and alignment check.

Question Alignment Rule: Must be testable, non-judgmental, and specifically target psychological variables (e.g., anxiety levels vs. social media consumption).
Hypothesis Alignment Rule: Directly predicts the directional relationship between Independent and Dependent variables based on prior research theory.
Ethical Safeguards Rule: Must guarantee informed consent, confidentiality, right to withdraw, and debriefing protocol (APA standards).
Montano Habit Connection: Habit 2 - Begin with the End in Mind. A proposal is your roadmap. Without alignment between question, method, and ethics, the research loses validity.

Phase II: Modeling (15 min) & Individual Work (20 min)

Formative Stage

15-Min Modeling: Exemplar Mini Research Proposal Alignment

Review the teacher's model below. Notice how every component logically flows into the next without contradictions.

1. Research Question: Does 15 minutes of mindfulness meditation reduce pre-exam physiological anxiety in high school seniors?
2. Hypothesis: Students who complete 15 minutes of guided meditation will score significantly lower on the State-Trait Anxiety Inventory (STAI) compared to a control group.
3. Method & Sample: Experimental design (Randomized Control Trial). Sample: 40 volunteers from 4th High School.
4. Instrument & Analysis: STAI standardized questionnaire + heart rate monitors. Data analysis via Independent Samples t-Test.
5. Ethical Safeguards: Parental consent forms obtained; right to withdraw at any time; anonymity protected via participant codes; full debriefing provided after testing.

Plenary & Research Proposal Assessment Checklist

Summative & Formative Evaluation

Use this interactive rubric to evaluate student proposal presentations during the plenary session or for final portfolio assessment.

Evaluation Criteria Exemplary (4 pts) Proficient (3 pts) Developing (2 pts) Score
1. Alignment & Logic Flawless alignment between question, hypothesis, method, & analysis. Minor gaps in logic; hypothesis generally supports question. Inconsistencies between question and methodology.
2. Ethical Rigor Comprehensive APA ethical protocols clearly outlined. Standard safeguards included (consent & debriefing). Ethical safeguards vague or missing critical elements.
3. Peer Feedback & Revision Actively incorporated peer feedback to enhance proposal quality. Reviewed peer feedback with minor edits made. Minimal participation in peer feedback process.
4. Defense / Plenary Clarity Communicates research rationale clearly; responds effectively to queries. Explains proposal clearly; answers most questions well. Struggles to justify methodological choices when questioned.
